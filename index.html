Who We Are

Shonga Farms LTD

Founded in 2015 by James Audu, Shonga Farms LTD has grown into a trusted name in agriculture across Kwara State and beyond. We combine traditional farming wisdom with modern techniques to deliver the finest crops, livestock, and animal products.

From our lush fields in Shonga to your table, we are committed to quality, sustainability, and community.

  • 100% Natural Produce
  • Ethically Reared Livestock
  • Fresh Eggs & Milk Daily
  • Supporting Local Farmers

What We Do

Our Services

We offer a complete range of agricultural services — from farm to fork.

Crop Production

We grow a variety of high-quality crops using sustainable practices — from grains to vegetables, all nurtured with care.

Livestock Rearing

Healthy, ethically raised cattle, goats, and poultry. Our livestock are free-range and raised with the utmost welfare standards.

Animal Products

Fresh eggs, rich milk, and other premium products sourced daily from our farm. Pure, natural, and nutritious.

Our Story

For over a decade, MARYPAULINE ALLIED ENTERPRISE has been dedicated to sustainable fish farming and poultry production in Shonga, Kwara State.

Our Journey

From 2015 to Today

MARYPAULINE ALLIED ENTERPRISE was born out of a passion for sustainable aquaculture and poultry farming. What started as a small family-run operation has grown into a thriving enterprise, integrating fish farming, chicken breeding, and the production of fresh eggs and poultry meat.

We remain deeply connected to our roots in Shonga, serving local communities and beyond with wholesome, ethically produced fish and poultry products.

Our commitment to quality, sustainability, and community has made us a trusted name in Kwara State and beyond.

Core Purpose

Mission & Vision

Guided by our commitment to quality, sustainability, and community.

Our Mission

To produce high-quality fish and poultry products using sustainable farming practices that protect the environment, support local economies, and nourish families with healthy, natural food.

Our Vision

To be a leading model of sustainable aquaculture and poultry farming in West Africa, inspiring a new generation of farmers and building a future where farming and nature thrive together.

Milestones

Our Timeline

Key moments that shaped MARYPAULINE ALLIED ENTERPRISE into what it is today.

2015

Foundation – MaryPauline establishes the enterprise in Shonga, Kwara State, starting with a few fish ponds and a chicken coop.

2017

Expansion – The farm expands to 20 ponds and increases poultry capacity, introducing modern feeding and health management systems.

2019

Diversification – Added egg production and began supplying fresh eggs and poultry meat to local markets.

2022

Community Impact – Launched a training program for local youth, teaching sustainable fish and poultry farming techniques.

2025

10th Anniversary – Celebrated a decade of growth, now operating a fully integrated fish and poultry farm with a loyal customer base.

Our Journey

From 2015 to Today

MARYPAULINE ALLIED ENTERPRISE was born out of a passion for sustainable agriculture and a vision to create a farm that not only produces food but also nurtures the land and the people who work on it.

What started as a small family-run operation has grown into a thriving poultry farm, livestock rearing, and the supply of fresh animal products like eggs and milk.

Today, we remain deeply connected to our roots in Shonga, serving local communities and beyond with wholesome, ethically produced food.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To produce high-quality livestock, and animal products using sustainable farming practices that protect the environment, support local economies, and nourish families with healthy, natural food.

To be a leading model of integrated, sustainable agriculture in West Africa, inspiring a new generation of farmers and building a future where farming and nature thrive together.

Milestones

Our Timeline

Key moments that shaped MARYPAULINE ALLIED ENTERPRISE into what it is today.

2015

Foundation – MaryPauline establishes MARYPAULINE ALLIED ENTERPRISE on a small plot in Shonga, Kwara State, with a handful of livestock and a vision for sustainable farming.

2017

Expansion – The farm expands to 50 acres, introducing crop production and building the first poultry houses for free-range chickens.

2019

Diversification – Added goats and dairy cattle, launching fresh milk and egg production for local markets.

2022

Community Impact – Established a cooperative program, supporting over 30 smallholder farmers with training and resources.

2025

10th Anniversary – Celebrated a decade of growth, now operating on over 120 acres, with a full range of farm products and a loyal customer base.
